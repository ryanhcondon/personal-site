About Me

Hi! I'm Ryan (I use they/them, but also comfortable with he or she!). I'm a math & strategy nerd who likes people just as much.

I'm currently competing as a world-class Magic: The Gathering player with team Sanctum of All, and writing about strategy and decision-making. I've had two runs at Qeepsake: first as its 4th employee building the early business processes, then returning for four years to lead growth marketing and rebuild the company's data pipeline as a Data Engineer. In between I spent a year at Wayfair as a data analyst. I have a degree in (and passion for!) mathematics from Washington University in St. Louis, with minors in computer science and psychology.

I'm looking for a high-agency, intellectually engaging environment to learn, grow, and solve interesting problems with kind & smart people.
