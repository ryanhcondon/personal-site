Team Platform · In Use

PT Hub

A multi-tool platform I built and run for my Pro Tour testing team. One account per person follows you across every season and every tool: a Deck Board where each teammate keeps a live probability split over the archetypes they might register, heat-mapped against the team average with full drift history; an Expenses splitter that weights a shared testing house by who stayed which nights and settles everyone in the fewest possible payments; and a Survey whose answers can feed those expense weights.

  • Node.js
  • Vanilla JS
  • Supabase
  • Vercel
